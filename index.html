Identifying the type

4 Day duties are set by number in the 4 Day duties panel. A duty with no relief is straight through. Any other duty with compass over the standard max (Mon–Sat) is a split.

Relief is the longest break in the duty (with 3 pieces, the short break is just a bus changeover).

    Single rota details

    Rota no.
    Rota moves Down: line 1 → 2 → 3 each week. Rest from Saturday to the next line's Sunday is checked when the rota moves.
    Week types Each line can also be set in the grid under its number. Any: no limit · Mix: E, M and no type · Early: E · Midday: M and no type · L/M: L and M · Late: L. Splits (S) only in Any, Mix, Early and Midday weeks.
    Split duties (S) weeks 0 = no limit (school and split rotas such as SCH). Counted over consecutive lines in rota order.
    Duty numbers of this rota
    Ranges, comma-separated, e.g. 201-399, 2401. Used for the Subset filter, duties on no rota, Auto-fill and Balance weeks.

    Global rota parameters

    OmniROTA stores only the duty value for Sunday 4 Day duties. Their pay value is the duty value with the 16% premium (× 1.16, marked * in the duty list). Save for OmniROTA writes this pay value into the .ORD file. Which duties are 4 Day is set by the 4 Day numbers under Duty check → Rules.

    Your own duty types
    CodeNameCounts asSign on from / beforeSign off from / before
    Code: 1–3 letters, e.g. N for night. Times are optional: with times, duties in that range get this type automatically (before E, M, L and S); without, you give it to duties by hand. "Counts as" decides how it matches week types and the split rule.

    Balance weeks:

    Keeps every RD and never touches weeks that are SP on every working day. Duties and the other SP days move between lines to get the lowest make up time: no week above the minimum while another is below it, no week over the maximum, and the minimum daily rest kept. Nothing changes if it cannot do better. Undo puts it back.

    With lines given, only those weeks swap duties among themselves. Lines you don’t balance keep their duties exactly as they are. The other weeks are not touched. Both can be used together.

    Rota line check and swap

    Click a day to select it (green), now . Move the lower line with ▲ ▼ or Jump to.

    Swap options Duties all week swaps only days where both lines work (RD and SP stay). Complete weeks swaps everything, RD and SP too.

    Auto-fill:

    Puts duties that are on no rota into the empty days of this rota, keeping the minimum daily rest and giving longer duties to the lines with the shortest weeks. If no empty day fits, the duty replaces an SP day. Weeks that are SP on every working day are never changed.

    Week types:

    Suggested from the duties

    Duties on this rota counted by their duty type, and how many Early, Midday and Late weeks they can fill.

    From the duties on each line (Walsall rule)

    Only E → Early · M (with or without untyped duties) → Midday · only untyped duties → Any · only L → Late · L and M → L/M · E with M or no type → Mix · others → Any. Splits fit Early, Midday and Mix; Sunday duties other than L and SP/RD do not count. You can change any line afterwards.

    Set lines by hand

    Give a week type to many lines at once, for example lines 2, 7, 12, 17 … as L/M. Line numbers are the numbers shown in the grid.

    Special days

    On the days you choose, every duty on the rota changes to SP, or to its special version with the letter in front: 2001 → E2001. If that duty is missing, already used or breaks the minimum rest, the most similar free special duty of the same sub rota’s subset is used (closest sign-on and sign-off); if none fits, SP. RD and SP stay. Load the special duties first with Load duties (.DIF / .DCL). Tip: use Copy job first to keep the normal rota.

    Days

    Bank Holiday Monday (BHM)

    On a Bank Holiday Monday the Sunday duties are worked on the Monday. This makes a copy of as a new rota job in which every sub rota's Monday duties are taken off and replaced by Sunday duties. The normal rota job stays as it is. Monday RD stays RD, Monday SP stays SP, each Sunday duty is used once and the minimum rest of each sub rota is kept.

    Rota statistics

    New sub rota

    Builds lines from the free duties of the chosen numbers (duties on no other rota). Rest days are spread so every day has enough lines; extra working days become SP. Then weeks are balanced.
