    Tools

    Click a cell, then click a duty in the list to put it there. Click a duty's type letter in the list to change it (E, M, L, S). Set each line's week type (Any, Mix, Early, Midday, L/M, Late) under its number. Use the Tag & type tab to change the type of many duties at once. Double-click or Enter to type. Keys: arrows move, R toggles RD, S enters SP, Delete clears, Ctrl+Z undoes.

    Single rota details

    Rota no.
    Rota moves Down: line 1 → 2 → 3 each week. Rest from Saturday to the next line's Sunday is checked when the rota moves.
    Week types Each line can also be set in the grid under its number. Any: no limit · Mix: E, M and no type · Early: E · Midday: M and no type · L/M: L and M (never splits) · Late: L.
    Split duties (S) weeks 0 = no limit (school and split rotas such as SCH). Counted over consecutive lines in rota order.
    Duty numbers of this rota
    Ranges, comma-separated, e.g. 201-399, 2401. Used for the Subset filter, duties on no rota, Auto-fill and Balance weeks.

    Global rota parameters

    Your own duty types
    CodeNameCounts asSign on from / beforeSign off from / before
    Code: 1–3 letters, e.g. N for night. Times are optional: with times, duties in that range get this type automatically (before E, M, L and S); without, you give it to duties by hand. "Counts as" decides how it matches week types and the split rule.

    Balance weeks:

    Keeps every RD and SP and moves the duties between lines so each week is within the minimum and maximum week value, with the minimum daily rest. Nothing changes if it cannot do better. Undo puts it back.

    Rota line check and swap

    Click a day to select it (green), now . Move the lower line with ▲ ▼ or Jump to.

    Swap options Duties all week swaps only days where both lines work (RD and SP stay). Complete weeks swaps everything, RD and SP too.

    Auto-fill:

    Puts duties that are on no rota into the empty days of this rota, keeping the minimum daily rest and giving longer duties to the lines with the shortest weeks.

    Week types:

    Give a week type to many lines at once, for example lines 2, 7, 12, 17 … as L/M. Line numbers are the numbers shown in the grid.

    Rota statistics

    New sub rota

    Builds lines from the free duties of the chosen numbers (duties on no other rota). Rest days are spread so every day has enough lines; extra working days become SP. Then weeks are balanced.
