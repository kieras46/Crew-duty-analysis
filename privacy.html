RET – Rota Editor Tool: privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

What the extension does with your data

RET – Rota Editor Tool reads the files you choose (OmniROTA .ORD, .ORL, .DCL, .RIF and .DIF files and crew duty summary PDFs) on your own computer. The files are not uploaded and are not sent to anyone, including the developer.

What is stored

Your jobs, rota jobs, garage settings and driver lists are saved in the extension's own storage in your Chrome browser, on your computer only. Removing the extension deletes them.

What is not collected

The extension does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal or usage data. It has no analytics, no advertising and no accounts. It asks for no Chrome permissions.

Network

The only network request is for the page fonts (Google Fonts); no file content or other data is sent with it. Without a connection the extension works with the system fonts.

Contact

Questions: through the support link on the extension's Chrome Web Store page.
